Data reduction experience

My expertise involves the end-to-end processing of spectroscopic and narrowband imaging data from raw observations to science-ready material. I am experienced in using IRAF and PyRAF to perform the main reduction steps, including flux calibration and, for near-IR observations, telluric correction. Currently, I am also expanding my expertise to ESO data reduction environments such as EsoRex and EsoReflex.
